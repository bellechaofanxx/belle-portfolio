01 / ABOUT ME

Content decisions.
Grounded in search.

I’m Joanna Belle Betonio, an SEO Content Strategist with a background in writing, optimisation, and leading content teams.

Working closely with writers and SEO specialists has shaped how I approach content. A keyword is a starting point: the useful decisions come from understanding search intent, reviewing the SERP, and looking at how existing pages perform.

My work has grown from drafting and editing into content planning, prioritisation, workflow management, and editorial decision-making. I connect search insights with practical direction—what to create, what to improve, and where the team’s effort can make the most sense.

I also build AI-assisted content workflows with editorial review at their core, giving writers a structured process from research and planning through to quality assurance.

What I did

Ranking movement reported from my work. This describes an observed result, rather than a guarantee of future performance.

The situation

A six-writer team needed a structured workflow to support high-volume SEO content production alongside an editorial review process.

What I did

I helped build and manage an AI-assisted workflow covering keyword research, planning, outlining, drafting, and quality assurance. My work included assigning topics, reviewing drafts, and guiding writers through the process.

The scale

The team’s production was approximately 40 articles per week. The focus was managing the process that supported that output, with editorial checks built into the workflow.

Approximate team production volume, not individual output or a traffic-growth metric.

The challenge

A growing content library had uneven performance. Some pages appeared in search but lacked engagement; others did not fully match the user’s intent.

My approach

I reviewed intent alignment, heading structure, contextual internal links, and performance signals such as impressions, CTR trends, and position ranges. I categorised and prioritised pages before optimising their content.

The deliverable

A practical prioritisation framework, clearer headings and sections, and a repeatable audit system for future updates.

Anonymised work example from my existing portfolio. Outcomes describe process improvements; no traffic uplift is claimed.

The challenge

The website needed a structured plan that organised content by topic relevance and search intent, with priorities based on potential impact.

My approach

I grouped keywords by topic and intent, identified pillar pages, and mapped supporting explainers, comparisons, and guides. Pillar content acted as the primary internal linking hub.

The deliverable

A content map with titles, formats, search intent, and priorities, supported by a clear decision framework that could be applied to other topics.

Adapted from my existing portfolio. Client and topic details are anonymised.

The challenge

As content volume increased, the team needed a consistent way to review SEO alignment, readability, and internal linking.

My approach

I standardised checks for title and H1 alignment, logical H2–H3 hierarchy, sections that answer user questions, relevant contextual links, and clear paragraph formatting.

The deliverable

A reusable on-page checklist to guide drafting, editing, and publication reviews across the content workflow.

Framework documented in my existing portfolio; presented without confidential client data.
